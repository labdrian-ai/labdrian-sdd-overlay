# Own receipt-driven development (RDD) on our reviewer and verifier

Objective: replace gentle-ai's native review with our own mechanically enforced lifecycle (binding, validated results, verifier verdicts, typed evidence, delivery check, stop hook, CI gate, Codex launcher) before removing gentle. Branches `feat/own-rdd-t<n>`, one PR per task, merged in order (stacked-to-main, memory "one slice at a time"). Test runner: `go test` from `engine/` and `tools/<tool>/`; writer `builder` (sonnet, max), independent verification `verifier` (opus, high).

## Specs

- **S1 — Mechanical guarantees.** Owner: "necesitamos garantias mecanicas pruebas evidencias enontces necesitamos todo lo que nos las pueda otorga". Every rule of the review contract that prose states today (`skills/role-chain/SKILL.md`, `roles/reviewer/body.md`, `roles/verifier/body.md`, `odd/review-results/README.md`) is checked by engine code with a test; a check that cannot run refuses, never passes. This reverses the cut recorded at `docs/architecture/role-design/per-role-reviewer.md:76` (no engine verb, no schema), by the owner's answer above.
- **S2 — Review-result contract.** Each reviewer finding carries a stable `id`. An engine domain package parses a reviewer result and refuses it when: the echoed `base`, `head` or `diff_sha256` differ from the binding; `inspection.status` is not `completed`; the reviewed paths differ from `git diff --name-only base..head`; a severity or disposition is outside its enum; an `id` repeats; a BLOCKER or CRITICAL finding has no proof reference.
- **S3 — Binding and storage.** `labdrian review bind` computes the full base and head SHAs and `diff_sha256`, creates the detached worktree `~/labdrian-sdd-overlay-worktrees/review-<head12>` and prints the binding; `labdrian review record` validates a result against the recomputed binding (S2) and writes it atomically, never overwriting, under `odd/review-results/<feature>/<head12>-<n>.json`, then prints the role-handoff skeleton (`payload_kind: review-report`, evidence `review-diff` and `review-result` with their sha256). Paths derived from input cannot escape `odd/review-results/`.
- **S4 — Verifier verdicts.** The verifier returns one structured verdict per finding id (`reproduced`, `not-reproduced`, `not-run`, with command, output and base result); the engine parses and stores it beside the result, and one pure function decides whether the review blocks, per `skills/role-chain/SKILL.md` (severe and reproduced, or unknown causality reproduced on head and not on base, blocks), with at most one correction round per candidate.
- **S5 — Typed evidence and delivery check.** `labdrian roles append` refuses a `review-report` record whose `review-diff` and `review-result` evidence is missing or whose sha256 differs from the stored files; `labdrian review check` recomputes `diff_sha256` for the head being delivered and refuses when no recorded, validated, non-blocking result binds it. The delivery role runs it. Owner (L15): "y buenos hay labros que no llevan review que ya cone l verifier basta por validacion del riesgo tambien"; design accepted in L16: `labdrian review exempt --feature F --base B --head H --risk <items>` reads the verifier's report on stdin and stores `<head12>-<n>.exempt.json` bound to the head's `diff_sha256`; it refuses a declared risk naming any high-risk item (1-6), a missing report, or a report that is not clear; `review check` clears a head with a non-blocking review result or a valid exemption bound to it, and a blocking review wins over any exemption. The risk is declared by the agent, not computed by the engine.
- **S6 — Stop hook of our own.** A Claude Code Stop hook installed by `labdrian install-hooks` detects a committed head on a non-default branch with no recorded result binding it and blocks the stop once per session and candidate with the exact `labdrian review bind` command; `status-hooks` reports it.
- **S7 — CI gate accepts our results.** `tools/archive-anchor-gate` accepts a recorded result under `odd/review-results/` bound to the archived head as the source of the approved tree, beside gentle receipts, so our lifecycle stands alone before gentle is removed.
- **S8 — Lens rows as data.** The reviewer's lens row sentences (default, readability, risk-line second launch) live once in a canonical file the parent copies from; the rows used are recorded in the result, so `review_lens_count` in `skills/_shared/actuals-record.schema.json:62` derives from our data, not gentle's `selected_lenses`.
- **S9 — Codex launcher with enforced read-only.** Decision D11 of `per-role-design-v3.md`: `labdrian review launch --runtime codex` runs the reviewer through `codex exec --sandbox read-only -m <model>` and captures its output for `record`; the v3 contradictions on Codex (`:72`, `:105`) are reconciled.
- **S10 — Pi verifier.** A verifier role file for Pi, generated like the other roles.
- Assumption (from the owner's "todo"): the S6 hook blocks once per candidate, like gentle's, rather than only reminding. Out of scope: removing gentle-ai review, its hooks, lens agents and `engine/reviewreceipt` (step 3 of the owner's order).
