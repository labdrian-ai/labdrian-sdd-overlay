# Phase 9 C25: a review of our own that beats gentle-ai's on recall of real defects and costs no more, or gentle-ai review comes back

Objective: Phase 9 item C25, placed before C22: measure first, then build a cheap tool-free lens transport, deterministic error analyzers and a "hunt" lens, and keep them only if the fixed bar of S1 is met on the grown corpus; otherwise re-enable gentle-ai review. Shaping branch `docs/phase9-c25-review-hunt`; task branches `feat/phase9-c25-t<n>`, one PR per task, stacked-to-main, merged in order (memory "one slice at a time").
Test runner: `go test` in `engine/` (the review packages and `engine/shelltest` for the bench scripts), `shellcheck` over the bench scripts; the bench itself is a live measurement with no runner (its numbers are evidence in the Log); writer `builder`, independent verification `verifier`.
