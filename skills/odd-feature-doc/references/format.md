# Worked examples of the feature document

## A task line

The box is the status, the route is `inline` or `delegated`, and the commit is `-` until the task is committed.

`- [ ] T1 — S1-S2 — inline — what it delivers — commit: -`

## The `shaped:` segment

A task the shaper designed carries the segment just before `commit:`:

`- [ ] T1 — S1 — delegated — what it delivers — shaped: L2 — commit: -`

## A Log entry

Each entry after `L1` holds a user correction verbatim, a decision or evidence. The foreman numbers its entries after the last entry of the Log and marks them with its role like the others:

`- **L7** (2026-11-16, foreman): the owner's words, verbatim, or the evidence observed.`
